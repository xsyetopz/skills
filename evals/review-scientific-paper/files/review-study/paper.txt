Checklist-Guided Code Review and Escaped Defects: A Controlled Study
R. Okafor, L. Brandt, S. Ishikawa
Journal of Illustrative Software Engineering 14(2), 2025. DOI: 10.5555/jise.2025.0142
Version of record published 2025-04-11.

Abstract
We compared checklist-guided review with unguided review in 40 student
teams. Checklist teams let fewer defects escape to the test phase.

4 Method
4.1 Participants. 40 teams of three final-year undergraduate students at a
single university, randomly assigned to checklist (n = 20) or unguided
(n = 20) review. No professional developers took part.
4.2 Task. Each team reviewed the same 1,200-line Java change set seeded
with 30 known defects. Escaped defects are seeded defects not flagged in
review.

5 Results
Table 2. Escaped defects per team (mean, SD, 95% CI)
  Condition     n    Mean   SD    95% CI
  Checklist     20   8.1    2.9   [6.7, 9.5]
  Unguided      20   10.9   3.6   [9.2, 12.6]

Checklist teams let 2.8 fewer defects escape on average (Cohen's
d = 0.86). The difference was significant, t(38) = 2.71, p = .010.
Review time did not differ, t(38) = 0.84, p = .41.

6 Threats to validity
All participants were students at one institution, the change set was a
single seeded Java file, and defects were seeded rather than naturally
occurring. We do not claim the effect transfers to professional teams.
