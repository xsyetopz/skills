# Pricing contract

Agreed with finance, 2026-05.

| Subtotal (before discounts) | Volume discount |
| --- | --- |
| below 100.00 | none |
| 100.00 up to 499.99 | 5% |
| 500.00 and above | 10% |

- Members get a further 2% off the amount after the volume discount.
- The total is rounded to whole cents, halves rounded up.
- A negative subtotal is rejected with ValueError.
