Blue-Light Glasses Improve Programmer Sleep Quality: A Randomized Trial
M. Varga, T. Holm. J. Illustr. Behav. Res. 2019. DOI: 10.5555/jcbr.2019.0077

Results. Programmers randomized to blue-light glasses (n = 62) reported
better sleep quality than controls (n = 60): PSQI difference 2.4 points,
95% CI [1.1, 3.7], t(120) = 3.60, p < .001 (Table 2).
Conclusion. Blue-light glasses are an effective sleep intervention for
programmers.
