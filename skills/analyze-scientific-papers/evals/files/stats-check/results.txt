Results (excerpt from the submitted manuscript)

Participants who received the reminder completed more lessons than the
control group, t(28) = 1.20, p = .03.
Completion differed across the three onboarding variants,
F(2, 57) = 1.10, p < .01.
Reminder recipients were more likely to finish the course,
chi2(1) = 4.10, p = .043.
Lessons completed correlated with prior experience, r(48) = .30, p = .034.
