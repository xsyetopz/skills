# Issue 118: Hold seats while the customer pays

Reported by: product (Dana)

When a customer picks seats for a show, we should hold them so nobody else
can take them while the customer is on the payment page. Right now two
customers can pay for the same seat and support has to refund one of them.

What we want:

- Picking seats puts them on hold for that customer.
- Other customers see held seats as unavailable.
- If the customer pays in time, the seats become theirs.
- If the hold runs out, the seats go back on sale.
- The customer can release the hold by leaving checkout.

Notes from the thread:

- Ops wants the hold length configurable per venue. Nobody has agreed on a
  default yet.
- The payment provider sometimes takes several seconds to confirm, so a
  payment can be in flight when the hold runs out. We have not decided
  what should happen then.
